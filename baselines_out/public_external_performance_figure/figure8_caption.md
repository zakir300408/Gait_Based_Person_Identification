**Figure 8 | Identification accuracy on public gait datasets.** (a) Window-level identification accuracy on the official WHU-Gait Dataset #3 split (26,283 enrollment and 2,991 test windows; 118 identities; unconstrained smartphone IMU). (b) Window-level identification accuracy on the Zou-preprocessed OU-ISIR split (13,212 enrollment and 1,409 test windows; 745 identities; waist-mounted IMU).
